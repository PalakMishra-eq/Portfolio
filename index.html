About me

I'm a PhD student affiliated to the NESt Lab at Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology, Delhi. My work spans distributed systems, networking for AI/ML workloads, and scalable software solutions.

And my research interests include Systems and Networks for Distributed AI/ML Workloads, Distributed & High-Performance Systems, Edge–Cloud Architectures, Mixture-of-Experts, Transport Protocols for HPC/AI Applications, 6G edge/RAN inferencing workloads.

Previously, I worked at Birlasoft and Cognizant, gaining professional experience in full-stack development, and contributed to the Mifos Initiative through Code for GovTech. I specialize in FullStack Development, Linux, Computer Networks, Systems architecture and Operating systems, and distributed systems technologies.
